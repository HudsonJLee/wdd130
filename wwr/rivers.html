Our Rivers

Legendary water. Unforgettable adventure.

The Heart of Every Trip

At Dry Oar Boating, every adventure begins with the river. We operate on some of the most spectacular waterways in the American West — rivers that carve through ancient canyons, rush beneath towering cliffs, and calm into glassy stretches where eagles soar overhead. Whether you are a first-time paddler or a seasoned rafter seeking the next challenge, our rivers have something for you.

We have spent decades learning these rivers in every season, at every water level, and in every kind of weather. That experience means you get the most thrilling and safest trip possible — guided by people who know these waters like their own backyard.

Where We Run

The Colorado River

The Colorado River is the crown jewel of western whitewater. Cutting through the spectacular walls of Glenwood Canyon, the Shoshone section delivers non-stop Class IV action through one of the most dramatic landscapes in North America. The canyon walls rise nearly 2,000 feet above the river, and the rapids keep coming with barely a break between them.

This stretch is recommended for guests who want a real adrenaline rush. Expect to get soaked, hold on tight, and leave with a grin you can't shake. Our guides are experts on this run and will prepare you fully before you ever hit the water.

  • Difficulty: Class III–IV
  • Length: 12.5 miles
  • Best Season: May through September
  • Minimum Age: 12 years
  • Trip Duration: 3–4 hours

The Roaring Fork River

For a gentler introduction to whitewater or a perfect family outing, the Roaring Fork River is an ideal choice. With a mix of mild rapids and calm scenic floats, this trip lets you soak in the beauty of Colorado's mountain valleys without the intensity of the canyon runs.

The Roaring Fork winds through lush meadows and cottonwood groves, offering excellent wildlife viewing along the way. Deer, herons, osprey, and the occasional fox can be spotted from the raft. It is the perfect blend of gentle excitement and pure relaxation.

  • Difficulty: Class I–II
  • Length: 8 miles
  • Best Season: April through August
  • Minimum Age: 5 years
  • Trip Duration: 2–3 hours

Choose Your Adventure

We offer trips designed for every kind of adventurer. Not sure which is right for you? Our team is happy to help you find the perfect fit.

Family Float

A mellow, scenic float perfect for kids as young as five. You will enjoy calm stretches, gentle riffles, and stunning scenery with a knowledgeable guide who keeps everyone safe and smiling.

Classic Whitewater

The full Dry Oar experience — big rapids, big fun, and expert guides who know every rock and wave on the run. Suitable for anyone in good health aged twelve and up, no prior experience necessary.

Overnight Expedition

Multi-day trips that combine whitewater with riverside camping, campfire cooking, and star-filled skies. The best way to truly disconnect and experience the wilderness of the canyon country.

Safety First, Always

Your safety is our top priority. Every Dry Oar guide is certified in swiftwater rescue, wilderness first aid, and CPR. We conduct thorough safety briefings before every trip and provide all the necessary equipment — life jackets, helmets, and wetsuits when conditions call for them.

Our fleet of rafts is inspected and maintained to the highest standards. We monitor river conditions daily and make conservative decisions about whether and how to run — because the river will always be there, and so will we.
