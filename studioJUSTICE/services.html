Common Questions

FAQ

How far in advance should I book?
For weddings and larger productions, 3–6 months is ideal to ensure date availability and proper pre-production time. For commercial and music video work, 4–6 weeks is a good baseline, though I can sometimes accommodate faster turnarounds. Reach out as early as possible — popular dates fill quickly.
Do you travel for projects outside Arizona?
Absolutely. I've traveled extensively for productions and I'm comfortable working anywhere in the country and internationally. Travel costs are calculated based on the destination and project scope and are outlined clearly in the project proposal before any commitments are made.
How long does post-production take?
Turnaround depends on project complexity. Music videos typically take 2–4 weeks from wrap to delivery. Documentary projects vary widely based on length and interview volume. Weddings are typically 6–10 weeks. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee.
What file formats do you deliver?
Final deliverables are provided as H.264 or H.265 MP4 files optimized for the platforms you need — YouTube, Instagram (16:9 and 9:16), Vimeo, or broadcast. ProRes or raw deliveries are available for clients who need them for further post-production work.
Do you offer revisions?
Yes. Every project includes two rounds of revision on the edit. Additional revisions are billed at an hourly rate. Color grade revisions are included in the first revision round. I've found that clear communication during the brief and pre-production phase almost always means the first cut is very close to the final product.
Can I use Studio Justice content for commercial purposes?
Commercial usage rights are included in commercial and brand content packages. For personal projects like weddings or music videos, personal use rights are standard. If you need expanded licensing for broadcast, advertising, or third-party distribution, this is easily added to any contract and outlined before production begins.
