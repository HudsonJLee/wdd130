Overview

Purpose

My website purpose is to market and show off my work to others so I can get more clients as a videographer. Studio Justice is a cinematic production studio offering music video production, documentary filmmaking, commercial content, narrative film, wedding videography, and event coverage. The site serves as a portfolio, a service guide, and a point of contact for potential clients.

Audience

Anyone looking for a videographer — specifically musicians building a visual brand, businesses that need commercial or brand content, couples planning their wedding, and event organizers who want professional film-quality documentation of their events. The audience is primarily located in the Phoenix, AZ area but extends nationwide and internationally.

About Hudson: I'm Hudson Justice, 21, from Arizona. I've always been drawn to the way light bends across a room, the way a fleeting gesture can hold a universe of feeling. I chase those moments with a camera in hand, not just to capture them, but to let them breathe, linger, and speak to anyone who sees them. Studio Justice isn't just a name — it's my middle name, my compass, the lens through which I see the world. Every project I touch is a chance to honor the subtle, the overlooked, the quietly powerful. I believe in intention: every frame, every cut, every shadow and highlight is a choice, and those choices are how stories take shape and hearts move. My work is colored by my love for vintage Italian elegance, the careful artistry of designer brands, and the way old and new can collide to make something timeless. I'm drawn to composition that feels effortless but is carefully considered, to movement that whispers rather than shouts, to moments that hold both nostalgia and possibility.

Behind the Scenes

The Creative Process: Every project at Studio Justice starts with a conversation — not about cameras or shot lists, but about feeling. I want to know what a client wants people to feel when they watch the final film. That emotional core becomes the compass for every decision that follows, from location choices to color grades to the rhythm of the edit. Before a single frame is captured, I spend significant time in pre-production: building mood boards, scouting locations, studying the light at different times of day, and writing out detailed shot lists that balance spontaneity with intentionality. I believe you can only improvise brilliantly when you know exactly what you're after. This preparation isn't just about efficiency on set — it's about creating space for magic. When the technical scaffolding is locked in, I'm free to be present, to notice the unexpected and lean into it.

Vision and Philosophy: I've always been drawn to films that are more felt than watched. Cinema at its best bypasses the analytical mind and speaks directly to something deeper — a memory, a longing, a sense of recognition. My visual language is shaped by a love of Italian cinema — the patience of the long take, the confidence of negative space, the way great directors let silence do the heavy lifting. I apply those principles whether I'm shooting a three-minute music video or a feature-length documentary. Color plays an enormous role in how I see storytelling. I spend as much time in the color suite as I do on set because a grade isn't just a stylistic finish — it's an emotional layer. Warm amber shadows can make a scene feel like memory; desaturated blues can make vulnerability feel clinical.

Gear and Equipment: The kit includes a Sony FX3 and Sony A7S III as primary cameras, Sigma Art Series primes (24mm, 35mm, 85mm), DJI RS 3 Pro gimbal, Aputure 300X and 120D II lighting, Rode NTG5 shotgun microphone and Wireless GO II lav system, DJI Mini 4 Pro drone, and DaVinci Resolve Studio plus Adobe Creative Suite for all post-production work.

The Production Process: Four steps from idea to final cut — Discovery Call, Pre-Production, Production Day(s), and Edit and Delivery — each described in detail to help clients understand what to expect at every stage of collaboration.

Services

Full Service Offerings: Detailed descriptions of all six production services: Music Video Production (concept development through delivery), Documentary Filmmaking (verite through broadcast master), Commercial and Brand Content (strategy alignment through usage licensing), Narrative and Short Film (script consultation through festival DCP), Wedding Videography (full-day coverage through raw archive), and Event Coverage (multi-camera through live streaming). Each service listing includes what's included, the deliverables clients receive, and a direct link to the contact form.

Why Studio Justice: Six differentiators that set the studio apart — Intentional Not Incidental, Collaborative by Design, Cinema-Level Quality, Clear Communication, Post-Production Expertise, and Available Worldwide. This section answers the question every potential client has: why hire Studio Justice instead of another videographer?

Who I Work With: The clients who get the most out of working with Studio Justice are musicians building a visual identity, businesses that understand story is the most powerful form of marketing, and couples who want a film that captures who they actually are. I work best with people who come to me with strong opinions and genuine stakes in the outcome. You don't need to know film — I know film. But you need to know your vision. The clearer you are about what you want people to feel, the better the film will be.
